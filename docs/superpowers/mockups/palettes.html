Design Review · Colour Direction

Four ways the dashboard could feel

Same layout, same real data (your league, GW1, 126 managers) — only the palette changes, so you're judging colour and mood, not four different designs. Pick one to take forward, or mix (e.g. "A's greens, C's readability"). Movement arrows and form lines are shown mid-season style to demonstrate the components; today everything is GW1.

A Pitch Broadcast B Electric Mint C Almanac (light) D Gunners
A · Pitch Broadcast Floodlit · Sporty Deep floodlit-night ground, vibrant grass green, warm gold for silverware. Saturday-afternoon-on-Sky energy. Reads "football" instantly.
B · Electric Mint Dark · Esports Your current mint, made intentional: true near-black, electric mint primary, a hot magenta reserved for "live / rising" signals. Modern, high-energy, evolves what you have.
C · Almanac Light · Editorial Warm cream and ink-navy, gold for winners, deep-green primary. Reads like a premium sports record-book. Deliberately unlike every dark FPL clone, ages well, most readable in daylight.
D · Gunners Club Identity Lean into the league name — charcoal, Arsenal red, gold. Tribal, "our league has a crest." Strongest identity, but ties the look to one club's colours.
